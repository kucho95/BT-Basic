outputs formatted (VCL)
Title: DECLARE FORMATTED RECEIVERS
(Mux systems only)
The outputs formatted function is an enhancement to the ouputs (VCL) function. It is used in the Declaration section of a VCL test to declare which pins on the board under test are to be formatted receivers. These pins cannot be received by the vectors and can be assigned states only in the timing sets.
Syntax
   outputs formatted <group id>
   outputs formatted <group id>,<group id>
   outputs formatted <group id>,<group id>, . . . . <group id>
      <group id> is a <label>
Parameters
<group id>
The identifier of the group of pins to be declared as formatted receiver pins.
Example
   ! in the Declaration section
         . . .
   assign Carry to nodes "U4-3"
   assign Q_Out to nodes "U4-4", "U4-6"
         . . .
         . . .
   outputs formatted Q_Out, Carry      ! formatted receivers
         . . .
