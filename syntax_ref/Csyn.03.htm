call (VCL)
Title: CALL VCL SUBROUTINE
The call function is used to invoke a digital subroutine in a VCL test. The call can optionally pass the vectors on which the subroutine is to operate. When call is executed, control transfers to the selected subroutine. When the exit is made from the subroutine, control returns to the function immediately following the call function which invoked that subroutine.
Subroutines can be nested; that is, a subroutine can itself contain a call to another subroutine.
Note that the call function is the only way to execute a subroutine.
Syntax
  call <sub id>
  call <sub id> (<vector id>)
  call <sub id> (<vector id>,<vector id>)
  call <sub id> (<vector id>,<vector id>, . . . . <vector id>)
     <sub id> is a <label>
     <vector id> is a <label>
Parameters
<sub id>
The identifier of the digital subroutine to be invoked.
<vector id>
The identifier of the vector to be passed to the subroutine. The vector must have been defined in the Vector Definition section of the digital test. A vector with a counter cannot be passed to a subroutine. If one subroutine calls another, it can pass its formal vectors to the called subroutine.
The number of vectors passed must be the same as the number of formal vectors listed in the subroutine's sub function. The passed vectors are assigned, in order, to the listed formal vectors. A maximum of 16 vectors can be passed.
Examples
  ! DEFINE VECTOR SUBROUTINES
          . . .
     sub Clock_cycle (Clock_hi, Clock_lo)
        execute Clock_hi
        execute Clock_lo
     subend
          . . .
  ! EXECUTE VECTORS
          . . .
     call Clock_cycle (Clk_A_hi, Clk_A_lo)   ! subroutine executes Clk_A_hi
          . . .                              ! and Clock_A_lo
     call Clock_cycle (Clk_B_hi, Clk_B_lo)   ! subroutine executes Clk_B_hi
          . . .                              ! and Clock_B_lo
