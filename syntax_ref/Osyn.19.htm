outputs (VCL)
Title: DEVICE OUTPUTS (RECEIVERS)
The outputs statements appear in the Declaration section of the test to indicate which groups of pins are outputs (system receivers) from the device or circuit under test. Once their direction as receivers is established, these groups cannot be changed during the test.
The same group cannot be declared in an outputs function and in an outputs formatted or outputs reference clock function in the same test.
See also the inputs and bidirectional statements.
Syntax
  outputs <group id>
  outputs <group id>,<group id>
  outputs <group id>,<group id>, . . . . <group id>
     <group id> is a <label>
Parameters
<group id>
The identifier of a group of pins to be declared as outputs. The group must have been defined in an assign to function.
Example
  assign Data_1 to device "U1" pins 3, 4, 5
  assign Carry to device "U1" pins 6
  assign Data_2 to device "U1" pins 7, 8, 9
  inputs Data_1
  outputs Data_2, Carry
General Information
Output pins not included in a vector, and not defaulted in an assign to function, default to receive‑X (off) when that vector is executed.
