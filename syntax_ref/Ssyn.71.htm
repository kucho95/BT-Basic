sub (VCL)
Title: VCL SUBROUTINE DECLARATION
The sub statement is used in the Vector Execution section of a digital test to declare the name of a subroutine and, if any, the names of its formal vectors. sub must be the first statement in the subroutine.
The formal vectors are variable vectors which can be referenced in the body of the subroutine. When the subroutine is called, the call statement passes to it the actual vectors which it is to execute.
If the VCL test has subroutines, they must be located at the beginning of the Vector Execution section, before any of the digital units. A VCL test can have any number of digital subroutines.
Syntax
  sub <sub id>
  sub <sub id> (<vector id>)
  sub <sub id> (<vector id>,<vector id>)
  sub <sub id> (<vector id>,<vector id>, . . . . <vector id>)
     <sub id> is a <label>
     <vector id> is a <label>
Parameters
<sub id>
The identifier of the subroutine.
<vector id>
The identifier of a formal vector. Any number of formal vectors can be specified, but limited by line length. Actual vectors will be assigned to the formal vectors when the subroutine is called (see the description of the call statement).
A vector which contains a counter cannot be passed to a digital subroutine.
Example
  sub Clock_cycle (Clock_hi, Clock_lo)
     execute Clock_hi
     execute Clock_lo
  subend
     ! calls for this subroutine will pass two vectors
  call Clock_cycle (Clk_A_hi, Clk_A_lo)
     ! subroutine executes vectors Clk_A_hi and Clk_A_lo
        . . .
  call Clock_cycle (Clk_B_hi, Clk_B_lo)
     ! subroutine executes vectors Clk_B_hi and Clk_B_lo
