inputs formatted clock (VCL)
Title: DECLARE THE DUT DRIVER
The inputs formatted clock function is an enhancement to the inputs (VCL) function. It is used in the Declaration section of a VCL test to declare which pin on the board under test is to be driven by the DUT clock. A VCL test can have only one DUT clock. However, the clock cannot be used if the testhead has only one module and the internal reference clock is required to synchronize test timing—the internal clock and the DUT clock are both derived from the same circuits in the module (see Reference Clock (TCLK) in Digital Testing).
The clock drives a squarewave to the board throughout the test. The clock frequency is determined by the dut clock period function.
Syntax
   inputs formatted clock <group id>
      <group id> is a <label>
Parameters
<group id>
The identifier of the pin group to be driven by the DUT clock.
Examples
   ! in the Declaration section
         . . .
   assign Board_Clock to pins 14
   assign Preset to pins 4, 6
         . . .
   inputs formatted Clear, Preset               ! formatted drivers
   inputs formatted clock Board_Clock           ! DUT clock driver
         . . .
   ! in the Timing section
   dut clock period 50n
