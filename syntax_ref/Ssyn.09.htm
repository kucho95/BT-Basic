scan cover extend (VCL)
Title: SPECIFY THAT A VCL TEST IS A COVER EXTEND TEST
The scan cover extend statement appears in the Declaration section of a VCL test and specifies a boundary-scan test type. This statement tell the VCL compiler that the test being compiled is a Cover-Extend test.
Syntax
  scan cover extend
Parameters
(none)
Example
     ! In the Declaration section
  scan cover extend                 ! Test J201
     . . .
  vector cycle 500n
  receive delay 400n
  assign TDI to nodes "BUFFER-1"
     . . .
General Information
This statement can only be used with the Cover-Extend option during runtime.
