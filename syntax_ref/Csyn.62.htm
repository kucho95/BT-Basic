cover extend supplies unmapped from (BT-Basic)
Title: UNMAP THE SPECIFIED SUPPLIES (COVER-EXTEND TESTS)
Use the cover extend supplies unmapped from statement to remove the ability of the fxoff and unpowered statements to disconnect the specified power supplies used in CET testing. (For more information, see the cover extend supplies mapped from statement).
Syntax
cover extend supplies unmapped from <supply>
   <supply> can be: <item>
                    <item>, <item>
                    <item>, <item>, . . ., <item>
   <item> can be: auxiliary <relay>
      <relay> can be: 1
                      2
                      3
                      4
                      5
Parameters
<supply>
The power supply used (can be auxiliary relay).
Examples
  cover extend supplies unmapped from auxiliary 5
