receive formatted (VCL)
Title: SET BIDIRECTIONAL PINS TO FORMATTED DRIVE
(Mux systems only)
The receive formatted statement is used in a timing set. It specifies which of the bidirectional formatted pin groups are to be receivers in that timing set.
Refer also to the description of the bidirectional formatted (VCL) statement.
Syntax
   receive formatted <group id>
   receive formatted <group id>,<group id>
   receive formatted <group id>,<group id>, . . . . <group id>
      <group id> is a <label>
Parameters
<group id>
The identifier of a bidirectional, formatted pin group.
Example
   ! in the Declaration section
            . . .
      assign Data1 to nodes "U6-9", "U12-9"
      assign Data2 to nodes "U11-7", "U21-7", "U31-7"
            . . .
      bidirectional formatted Data1, Data2
            . . .
   ! in the Timing section
            . . .
      timing set T3 is 10 events
         drive vector at event 2
         receive vector at event 8
            drive formatted Data2        ! bidirectional pins to be driven
            receive formatted Data1      ! bidirectional pins to be received
           at event 0 set Data2 to "010"
           at event 3 set Data2 to "011"
           at event 7 set Data1 to "01"
           at event 9 set Data2 to "111"
      end timing set
            . . .
   ! in the Vector Definition section
            . . .
      vector V001
         use timing set T3               ! T3 controls Data1 and Data2
            . . .
      end vector
            . . .
   ! in the Vector Execution section
            . . .
      execute V001
            . . .
