bidirectional formatted (VCL)
Title: DECLARE BIDIRECTIONAL NODES FOR TIMING SETS
(Mux systems only)
The bidirectional formatted function is an enhancement to the bidirectional (VCL) function. It is used in the Declaration section of the test to declare pins which are to be both bidirectional and formatted (i.e., controlled by timing sets).
When a bidirectional pin is to be driven by a timing set, it must be declared as a driver in that set (see the drive formatted function); when the pin is to be received, it must be declared as a receiver (receive formatted function).
Syntax
   bidirectional formatted <group id>
   bidirectional formatted <group id>,<group id>
   bidirectional formatted <group id>,<group id>, . . . . <group id>
      <group id> is a <label>
Parameters
<group id>
The identifier of a group of pins.
Examples
   ! in the Declaration section
         . . .
      assign Control to nodes "U1-4", "U1-6"
         . . .
         . . .
      bidirectional formatted Control
