drive formatted (VCL)
Title: SET BIDIRECTIONAL PINS TO FORMATTED DRIVE
(Mux systems only)
The drive formatted function is used in a timing set. It specifies which of the bidirectional formatted pin groups are to be drivers in that timing set.
Refer also to the description of the bidirectional formatted (VCL) function.
Syntax
   drive formatted <group id>
   drive formatted <group id>,<group id>
   drive formatted <group id>,<group id>, . . . . <group id>
      <group id> is a <label>
Parameters
<group id>
The identifier of a bidirection, formatted pin group.
Examples
   ! in the Declaration section
            . . .
      assign Data1 to nodes "U6-9", "U12-9"
      assign Data2 to nodes "U11-7", "U21-7", "U31-7"
            . . .
      bidirectional formatted Data1, Data2
            . . .
   ! in the Timing section
            . . .
      timing set T3 is 10 events
         drive vector at event 2
         receive vector at event 8
            drive formatted Data1          ! bidirectional pins to be driven
            receive formatted Data2        ! bidirectional pins to be received
         at event 0 set Data1 to "010"
         at event 3 set Data1 to "011"
         at event 9 set Data2 to "111"
      end timing set
            . . .
