listm (edit)
Title: LIST THE MARKED AREA OF THE WORKSPACE
The listm function (list marked) prints the area of the workspace marked by the mark softkey. The printout goes to the default print device, unless the default has been changed by a printer is function.
Line numbers are printed to the left of each line, as shown in the example below.
Lines longer than 72 characters are split into two lines when printed.
Syntax
  listm
Parameters
(none)
Example
     ! Line one
     ! Line two
     ! Line three   <‑‑‑‑‑‑‑
     ! Line four          marked area
     ! Line five    <‑‑‑‑‑‑‑
     ! Line six
  listm
     ! Prints    3: Line three
                 4: Line four
                 5: Line five
General Information
The listm function is not programmable. Also see the list and listn statements.
