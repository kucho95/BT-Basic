wait terminated when (VCL)
Title: SPECIFY THE STATES THAT TERMINATE HARDWARE WAITS
The wait terminated when function is used in the Declaration section of a VCL test to terminate a hardware wait. The function specifies, for one group of wait lines, the states that will terminate the wait.
There can be more than one wait terminated when function in a VCL test. If there are, their triggering conditions will be OR'd in determining when to terminate the wait—that is, when any of the groups of wait lines has its specified trigger states, then the wait will terminate.
It takes about 550 ns to restart once the triggering conditions are met. Therefore, if the conditions are already met when the wait keyword is executed, the wait will occur and will last (about) 550 ns.
Syntax
  wait terminated when <group id> is <states>
     <group id> is a <label>
     <states> is a <string constant>
Parameters
<group id>
The identifier of the group of pins whose states are to terminate the wait. The group must have been declared in a wait line function.
<states>
The state(s) on the group of wait lines that will terminate the wait. A state must be either 0 or 1; no other characters are allowed.
Example
  ! Declaration Section
     . . . .
  assign Continue to pins 9, 4
  assign Wait_bar to pins 17
     . . . .
  wait line Continue, Wait_bar
  wait terminated when Continue is "01"
  wait terminated when Continue is "10"
  wait terminated when Wait_bar is "0"
     . . . .
  ! Execution Section
     . . . .
  execute V0
  execute V1 wait       ! waits until any one of the trigger conditions is met
  execute V2
     . . . .
