variable to groups (VCL)
Title: ASSIGN VARIABLE STATES TO A GROUP OF PINS
The variable to groups function is used in the Declaration section to assign a numeric variable to a group of pins. Variables are passed to the test when it is executed and must contain integer values. Each integer is saved as a 32-bit, twos-complement number. The bits in the number are assigned to the pins in the specified group by vector execution statements (count, execute, and preset counter) that reference the variable. All values are binary and are not affected by format statements.
Syntax
  variable <variable> to groups <group id>
     <variable> can be:   <variable id>
                          <array id>(*)
     <group id> and <variable/array id> are <labels>
Parameters
<variable>
The name of the variable to be assigned. This can be a simple variable or a single-dimensioned array. Single array elements cannot be passed to the test. All arrays in VCL are zero-based.
<group id>
The name of the group of pins that will be driven or received by the states in the variable by vectors that reference that variable.
<label>
This is the same as a BT-Basic <label>. It can contain up to 32 characters, but with no spaces. The first character must be an uppercase letter and the remaining characters can be letters, numbers, or underscores (_).
Example
  ! in the Declaration section of the test
  test digital; A(*), B(*)
        . . .
     assign In to pins 1, 2, 3
     assign Out to pins 7, 9, 11
        . . .
     inputs In
     outputs Out
        . . .
     variable A(*) to groups In      ! assigns variable to pins 1, 2, 3
     variable B(*) to groups Out     ! assigns variable to pins 7, 9, 11
        . . .
  ! in the Vector Execution section
     . . .
  unit
     execute V1 drive A(1)
     execute V2 drive A(4)
     execute V3 drive A(4) receive B(4)
     execute V4 drive A(0)
     execute V1 receive B(2)
     execute V4 drive A(12) receive B(12)
        . . .
  end unit
