connect keep (ANALOG)
Title: CONNECT PORTS AND KEEP THE CONNECTION
Use the connect keep function in analog functional and mixed tests to connect any of the AccessPlus Card ports (Mux systems) or balanced ports (Utility Card) to nodes when the connection is to be maintained until it is explicitly opened with the disconnect (ANALOG) function.
The connect keep function has a settling delay to allow the relays to settle.
• Connect/Keep Access Ports (Mux systems)
• Connect/Keep Balanced Ports (Utility Card)
