supplies fixed (CONFIGURATION)
Title: FIXTURE POWER SUPPLY CONFIGURATION
The supplies fixed statement enables the fixture power supplies (12 VDC @ 1 A and 5 VDC @ 2 A) that are available with the ASRU Revision N Card. This statement is used in the board configuration file.
The fixture power supplies are controlled by BT-Basic commands (e.g. in the testplan).
A maximum of one 5 V power supply and one 12 V power supply can be configured in a module.
Syntax
supplies fixed <logicID> <voltage> volts to asru channel <channelNum>
   <logicID> is an integer from 101 to 108
   <voltage> can be: 5
                     12
   <channelNum> is either 101 or 102
Parameters
<logicID>
The power supply number (from 101 to 108); it must be unique across the modules.
<voltage>
The ASRU card can provide fixture power supply of 12 VDC @ 1 A and 5 VDC @ 2 A.
<channelNum>
The power supply channel. Use the following:
• 101 for 5 V power supply
• 102 for 12 V power supply
Examples
In module 3:
  supplies fixed 101 5 volts to asru channel 101
  supplies fixed 102 12 volts to asru channel 102
In module 2:
  supplies fixed 103 5 volts to asru channel 101
  supplies fixed 104 12 volts to asru channel 102
