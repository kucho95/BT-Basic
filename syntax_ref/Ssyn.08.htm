scan connect (VCL)
Title: SPECIFY THAT A VCL TEST IS A BOUNDARY-SCAN CONNECT TEST
The scan connect statement appears in the Declaration section of a VCL test and specifies a boundary-scan test type. This statement tell the VCL compiler that the test being compiled is an advanced boundary-scan connect test.
Syntax
  scan connect
Parameters
(none)
Example
     ! In the Declaration section
  scan connect                       ! For a device in the chain
     . . .
  vector cycle 200n
  receive delay 100n
  assign TDI to nodes "BUFFER-1"
     . . .
General Information
This statement can only be used with the InterconnectPlus software option.
