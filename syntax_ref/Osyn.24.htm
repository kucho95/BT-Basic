overdrive power (SAFEGUARD)
Title: OVERDRIVE POWER PER PIN
The overdrive power function appears in a SAFEGUARD file to specify the worst case power absorbed by any one of a device's outputs when that output is overdriven. The function can optionally describe the power actually dissipated in the associated junction. When compiling the executable digital test, the compiler uses this information for SAFEGUARD analysis.
Syntax
  overdrive power <watts 1>
  overdrive power <watts 1>,<watts 2> dissipated by heat source
     <watts 1> and <watts 2> are <numeric constants>
Parameters
<watts 1>
The worst case power (in watts) absorbed by any overdriven output pin on the device.
Default: 2.88
Range: 0.001 to 10000
<watts 2>
The power actually dissipated by the heat source (IC junction); must be less than or equal to <watts 1>.
Default: the value supplied for <watts 1>
Range: 0.001 to 10000
Example
  overdrive power 135m                 ! <watts 2> is also 135m watts
  overdrive power 0.35                 ! <watts 2> is also 0.35 watts
  overdrive power 0.35,5m dissipated by heat source
