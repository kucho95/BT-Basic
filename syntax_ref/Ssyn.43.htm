short (SHORTS)
Title: SPECIFY NODES SHORTED TOGETHER
The short statement specifies nodes which are shorted together. An opens test will be performed on the nodes to make sure they are shorted.
Syntax
  short <node name> to <node name>
  short <node name> to <brc>
  short <brc> to <brc>
     <node name> is a <string constant>
     <brc> is a <numeric constant>
Parameters
<node name>
The name of the node, as entered in the i3070 In-Circuit Test Software, specified as a short to another node.
<brc>
The brc format of the node name.
Example
  failure "Look for open fuse in FB-1."
  threshold 12
  short "FB-1.1L" to "FB-1.1R"
  short "FB-1.2L" to 10913
  short 10913 to 10915
