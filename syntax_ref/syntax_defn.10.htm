Delimiter
A delimiter is punctuation used to separate two elements or groups of elements in a statement. The most commonly used delimiters are a space and a comma. Other delimiters are the semicolon, colon, and percent symbol. A vertical bar separates statements on one line; the bar can be typed either as a bar or as a reverse‑slash (\). When the system processes the line, it automatically converts the slash to a bar.
