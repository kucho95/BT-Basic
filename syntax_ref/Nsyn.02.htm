nandtree (VCL)
Title: NAND TREE DEVICE TEST
(Mux systems only)
The nandtree statement appears in the Declaration section of the test to indicate that the device to be tested contains a NAND tree testability structure.
The nandtree statement provides information to the program generators for completing the Vector Execution section of the test.
The nandtree statement must precede the assign to statements in the test.
