inputs (VCL)
Title: DEVICE INPUTS (DRIVERS)
The inputs statements appear in the Declaration section of the test to indicate which groups of pins are inputs (i.e., system drivers) to the device or circuit under test. Once their direction as inputs is established, these groups cannot be changed during the test.
The same group of pins cannot be declared both in an inputs function and in an inputs formatted function in the same test.
See also the outputs and bidirectional statements.
Syntax
  inputs <group id>
  inputs <group id>,<group id>
  inputs <group id>,<group id>, . . . . <group id>
     <group id> is a <label>
Parameters
<group id>
The identifier of a group of pins to be declared as inputs. The group must have been defined in an assign function.
Examples
  assign Data_1 to device "U1" pins 3, 4, 5
  assign Data_2 to device "U1" pins 7, 8, 9
  assign In to device "U1" pins 11, 15
  inputs Data_1, In
  outputs Data_2
General Information
Input pins not included in a vector, and not defaulted in an assign to function, default to drive‑Z (high impedance) when that vector is executed.
