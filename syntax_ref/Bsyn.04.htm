bank/end bank (CONFIGURATION)
Title: TESTHEAD BANK CONFIGURATION
The bank and end bank statements delimit a bank configuration block in the testhead and board configuration files. The bank and end bank statements outline the outermost block of the nested block structure of the testhead configuration file. For every bank function used, in the testhead configuration file, there must be a matching end bank function.
Figure B-1 Modules in the testhead
Syntax
  bank <bank number>
     <bank number> can be:   1
                             2
  end bank
Parameters
<bank number>
The testhead contains one or two banks, depending on the model.
Examples
  bank 1
     module 1
        cards 1 asru n revision
        cards 2 to 5 hybrid high accuracy double density
        cards 6 control xtpb
        cards 7, 9 to 11 hybrid high accuracy double density
     end module
  end bank
