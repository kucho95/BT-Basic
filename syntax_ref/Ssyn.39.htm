settling delay (SHORTS)
Title: SETTLING DELAY
The settling delay statement specifies maximum the length of time between the application of a stimulus and the end of the measurement. The settling delay allows for the time required for reactive devices, such as capacitors and inductors, to reach their steady state. The detector does a measurement as soon as the source and relays are settled. If the test passes, it does not wait the entire specified settling time. If the test does not pass initially, the detector will continue sampling until the test passes or the settling time is reached.
The settling delay begins when the necessary measurement relays have settled. The total time spent taking a reading is, therefore, the sum of the relay settling time and the settling delay.
The i3070 In-Circuit Test Software will provide settling delays; a settling delay remains in effect until another settling delay is specified later in the file. The range of the delay is from 0 to 3.27 seconds. If a settling delay is not specified, a delay of 0 sec. will be used.
Syntax
  settling delay <delay>
     <delay> is a <numeric constant>
Parameters
<delay>
A numeric constant which specifies the delay interval in seconds.
The range of the delay is from 0 seconds to 3.27 seconds.
Example
  threshold 80
  settling delay 60u

  nodes "INPUT"
  nodes "R2‑2"
