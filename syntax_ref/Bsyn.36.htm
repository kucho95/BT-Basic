bond wire (SAFEGUARD)
Title: BOND WIRE SIZE
The bond wire function appears in a SAFEGUARD file to specify the size (i.e., length and diameter) of a device's bond wires. When compiling the executable digital tests, the compiler uses this information for SAFEGUARD analysis.
Syntax
   bond wire <length> by <diameter>
     <length> and <diameter> are <numeric constants>
Parameters
<length>
The length of the bond wire in micrometers.
Default: 2540
Range: 0.001 to 50000
<diameter>
The diameter of the bond wire in micrometers.
Default: 25.4
Range: 0.001 to 50000
Examples
   bond wire 2700 by 25.4
