sps (BT-Basic)
Title: SET UP DUT POWER SUPPLIES
The sps statement sets the operating voltage and current limit of the specified DUT supply. In addition, you can specify a wait to allow the applied voltage to settle; if you do not specify a wait, the system uses 30 ms. The sps statement can only be executed in powered mode, and after a cps statement (which connects all DUT supplies to the testhead). The dps statement disconnects DUT supplies.
The optimize keyword improves board test throughput by instructing the system to:
• turn on the supplies simultaneously and combine wait times.
• use the store/recall feature of the 6634 power supply.
See the following examples for more information on optimizing power supplies.
 
 
A voltage level equal to, or exceeding, 60 Vdc supplied to the board under test might present an operator safety hazard.
The programmable power supplies used in the Keysight ICT system are capable of supplying more than 60 Vdc to the board under test. If 60 Vdc or more is supplied to the board under test, safe operation requires that a fixture cover with a safety interlock be installed.
If the test and fixture apply power to electrical or electronic devices that can be explosive when defective or mis-loaded on the board under test, you should take all necessary safety precautions to protect the board test operator. This might include installing the interlocked‑fixture‑cover mentioned above.
Consult with your Safety Officer to ensure compliance with your facility safety requirements.
Syntax
  sps <ps_number>,<voltage>
  sps <ps_number>,<voltage>; optimize
  sps <ps_number>,<voltage>, <options>
  sps <ps_number>,<voltage>, <options>; optimize
     <ps_number> is an <integer> from 1 to 32
     <options> can be:   <limit>
                         <slew>
                         <wait>
                         <limit>, <slew>
                         <limit>, <wait>
                         <slew>, <wait>
                         <limit>, <slew>, <wait>
        <wait> is wa <time>
     <voltage>, <limit>, <slew>, and <time> are <numeric expressions>
Parameters
<ps_number>
An integer that identifies the power supply (1 to 32).
<voltage>
The voltage output (V) of the specified supply. The range depends upon the selected supply.
<limit>
The maximum allowable current output (A) of the specified supply. The range depends upon the supply. The default is 1 A.
<slew>
For DUT power supply models 6751 and 6752 only:
The output voltage slew rate in volts per second. The slew rate can be set to any value between 0 and 9.9E+37 (default). For very large values, the slew rate will be limited by the analog performance of the output circuit.
If other DUT power supply models are used (6624, 6621, 6634, 6642, 6773, 6746 or 5747), this option will be ignored. After acquiring the testhead, the slew rate of all outputs of these power supplies will be set to the default value of 9.9e+37.
<wait>
An option which establishes a wait period for the applied voltage to settle. <time> defines the wait period in seconds. The wait range is 0 to 9.999 seconds and the default is 30 ms.
Example
sps 1, 4, 0.5, slew 1000, wa 50m
Set DUT supply number six to five volts and one amp current limit:
  cps
  sps 6,5,1,wa 200m; optimize
 
Turn on supplies 1, 2, and 3 simultaneously with one 30 msec. wait.
  sps 1, 4; optimize
  sps 2, 5; optimize
  sps 3, 10; optimize
 
Turn on supplies 1, 2, and 3 simultaneously with one 50 msec. wait.
  sps 1, 4; optimize
  sps 2, 5, wa 50m; optimize
  sps 3, 10; optimize
 
Turn on supplies 1 and 2 simultaneously and wait 30 msec., then turn on supply 3 and wait 50 msec., then turn on supplies 4 and 5 simultaneously and wait 30 msec.
  sps 1, 4; optimize
  sps 2, 5; optimize
  sps 3, 5, wa 50m
  sps 4, 20; optimize
  sps 5, 20; optimize
