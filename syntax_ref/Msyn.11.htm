mod (BT-Basic)
Title: MODULUS
The modulus operator calculates the modulus of two quantities: that is, the remainder resulting from dividing the quantity on its left by the quantity on its right. The priority is 6.
Syntax
<numeric expression> mod <numeric expression>
Parameters
<numeric expression>
The quantities to be operated on.
Example
  print 12 mod 7                ! prints    5
  print 30 mod 6                ! prints    0
  print 3+18 mod 5              ! prints    6
     ! (mod has higher priority than +)
  print 3*18 mod 5              ! prints    4
     ! (* and mod have same priority)
  print 30 mod 4                ! prints    2
  print ‑30 mod 4               ! prints    ‑2
  print 30 mod (‑4)             ! prints    2
  A = 25 \ print A mod 7;A      ! prints    4 25
  print 45.68 mod 6.32          ! prints    1.44
General Information
Leave a space between the keyword mod and the first expression or errors can occur. For example, Amod . . . (instead of A mod . . .) looks like a variable; and 5mod . . . will cause an error because the m will be interpreted as a suffix (milli) to the constant (as in 5m for .005).
