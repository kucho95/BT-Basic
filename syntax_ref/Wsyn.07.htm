wait line (VCL)
Title: DECLARE THE WAIT NODES
The wait line function is used in the Declaration section of a VCL test to designate the output pins from the device or board under test that are to act as trigger lines to terminate hardware waits.
The wait lines are assigned to the six event trigger pins on the Module Control Card in the master module in the testhead. These pins are multiplexed 2:1. Regardless of the number of modules in the testhead, any one VCL test can have up to three nodes connected to wait lines.
Syntax
  wait line <group id>
  wait line <group id>, <group id>
  wait line <group id>, <group id>, <group id>
     <group id> is a <label>
Parameters
<group id>
The identifier of a group of pins to be declared as wait lines. A maximum of three pins can be declared in the test.
Example
  ! in the Declaration section
        . . .
     assign Continue to pins 9, 4
     wait line Continue
     wait terminated when Continue is "01"
        . . .
  ! in the Vector Execution section
        . . .
     execute V0
     execute V1 wait     ! waits until Continue goes to "01"
     execute V3
General Information
It takes about 550 ns to resume the test after the triggering states are received. Therefore, if the states are already met when the wait keyword is executed, the wait occurs and lasts for (about) 550 ns.
