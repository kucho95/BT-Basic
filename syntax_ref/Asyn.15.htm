andtree (VCL)
Title: AND TREE DEVICE TEST
(Mux systems only)
The andtree statement appears in the Declaration section of the test to indicate that the device to be tested contains an AND tree testability structure.
The andtree statement provides information to the program generators for completing the Vector Execution section of the test.
The andtree statement must precede the assign to statements in the test (see Digital Testing).
