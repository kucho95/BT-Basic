inputs formatted (VCL)
Title: DECLARE FORMATTED DRIVERS
(UnMux systems only)
The inputs formatted function is an enhancement to the inputs (VCL) function. It is used in the Declaration section of a VCL test to declare which pins on the board under test are to be driven by formatted drivers. These pins cannot be driven by the vectors and can be assigned states only in the timing sets.
Syntax
   inputs formatted <group id>
   inputs formatted <group id>,<group id>
   inputs formatted <group id>,<group id>, . . . <group id>
      <group id> is a <label>
Parameters
<group id>
The identifier of the group of pins to be driven as formatted pins.
Examples
   ! in the Declaration section
         . . .
   assign Clear to nodes "U1-3"
   assign Preset to nodes "U1-4", "U1-6"
         . . .
         . . .
   inputs formatted Clear, Preset ! formatted drivers
         . . .
