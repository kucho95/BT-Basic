halt (VCL)
Title: HALT DEVICE TEST
The halt function is used in the Vector Execution section of a digital test to stop the test and return control to the testplan. Before halting the test, the drivers are three‑stated, and the digital timeout timer is disabled. The test can be continued from the point at which it stopped by a (BT-Basic) test cont function; however, the digital timeout remains disabled. The halt function cannot be used in tests which specify the DUT clock (described in Advanced Testing With VCL in Digital Testing).
The test automatically halts at the end of the test or if digital timeout occurs; however, a test which halts because of a timeout cannot be continued. See also the pause (VCL) function.
 
 
You must re‑execute the necessary disabling vector and re-initialize the device after a halt.
Syntax
  halt
Parameters
(none)
Examples
  unit
        . . .
     halt
        . . .
  end unit
General Information
The SAFEGUARD safety analysis routines make use of halt intervals in the following way: The estimated execution time of a digital test is the maximum execution time observed in the test between halts. That is, if a test has two halts (three segments), the estimated execution time for safety analysis is the estimated execution time of the segment with the longest duration.
