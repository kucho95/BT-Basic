end controllerloop (VCL)
Title: END A CONTROLLER LOOP
The end controllerloop function is used in a digital unit to mark the end of a controller loop; the start of the loop is marked by the controllerloop function. Refer to the description of the controllerloop function for a description of the loops.
Syntax
  end controllerloop
Parameters
(none)
Examples
  ! in the Vector Execution section
     . . .
     unit
     . . .
        controllerloop     ! Measure voltage on TP12
           execute V1
           execute V2
           end controllerloop
        execute V3
        . . .
     end unit
     . . .
