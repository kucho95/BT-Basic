continue analog (VCL)
Title: CONTINUE EXECUTING THE ANALOG TEST
The continue analog function is used in the digital part of a mixed test to pass control from the VCL test to the analog test. The function pauses the digital test—that is, the drivers maintain their current states and the DUT clock, if any, continues to run. The function can be used as often as required in the test.
The analog part of the mixed test can have a similar function, continue digital, to pass control back to the digital test.
