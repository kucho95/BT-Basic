qstats (BT-Basic)
Title: INVOKE PUSHBUTTON Q-STATS
The qstats function is executed from the basic mode to invoke the Pushbutton Q‑STATS quality management software.
Syntax
  qstats
Parameters
(none)
Example
  qstats     ! Pushbutton Q-STATS appears in a new window.
