disconnect-unpowered (ANALOG)
Title: DISCONNECT BUSES FROM NODES
The disconnect-unpowered function is used within an analog in-circuit test block to disconnect specified buses from specified nodes.
The disconnect all form of the function opens all analog relays. It does not open GP relays.
The disconnect-unpowered function includes a delay to allow the relays to settle.
See the syntax description of the following statements:
• connect-unpowered (ANALOG)
• connect-powered (ANALOG)
• disconnect-powered (ANALOG)
• clear connect-powered (ANALOG)
• clear connect-unpowered (ANALOG)
Syntax
  disconnect all
  disconnect <node>
  disconnect <node>,<node>
  disconnect <node>,<node>, . . . . <node>
  disconnect <connection>
  disconnect <connection>;<connection>
  disconnect <connection>;<connection>; . . . . <connection>
     <connection> can be:   <bus> from <node>
                            <bus> from <node>,<node>
                            <bus> from <node>,<node>, . . . . <node>
        <bus> can be:   s    a
                        i    b
                        g    l
                        gl
        <node> can be a <node id> or <brc>
           <node id> is a <string constant>
           <brc> is a <numeric constant>
Parameters
 
<node id>
A string constant identifying the node from which the specified bus is to be disconnected. The node name must be the same one entered for that node in i3070 In-Circuit Test Software.
<brc>
A numeric constant describing the physical location (coordinate) of the module pin that is connected to the specified node. A coordinate consists of five numeric characters that are expressed in a brc (Bank Row Column) format:
• the first numeric character (B) designates the testhead bank, either 1 or 2. Bank 1 consists of the two modules on the right side of the testhead, and bank 2 is the two modules on the left side (as viewed from the front of the testhead).
• the next two numeric characters (R) designate the X-axis row, beginning with row 01 at the rear of the testhead and ending with row 23 at the front of the testhead (leading zeros are required). Note that row 12 is unused.
• the last two numeric characters (C) designate the Y-axis column, beginning with column 01 at the right side of the bank and ending with column 78 at the left side of the bank (leading zeros are required).
<bus>
Identifies the bus that is to be disconnected from the specified node. Note that only the G bus allows disconnections form more than one node.
Examples
! A simple in-circuit resistor test.
     disconnect all
     connect s to "R1-1"
     connect i to "R1-2"
     resistor 10k, 10, 10
! Move the i bus to make a second measurement in the same in-circuit test block.
     disconnect all
     connect s to "R1-1"
     connect i to "R1-2"
     connect g to "Node_6", "Node_7"
     resistor "R1", 5k, 10, 10
     disconnect i from "R1-2"; g from "Node_6", "Node_7"
     connect i to "R2-2"
     resistor "R2", 5k, 10, 10
General Information
Be careful when using brc locations, instead of node names, to avoid multiplexing conflicts.
Note that the G and L buses are the only two buses that can be disconnected simultaneously with the disconnect function.
