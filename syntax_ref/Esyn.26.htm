end pcf (VCL) (ITL)
Title: END OF PCF BLOCK
The end pcf function marks the end of a PCF block in the Vector Execution section of the VCL test (see the pcf function for a description of the PCF block).
In ITL, this is used in the disables section of any of the chain tests and/or in the silicon nail type test as part of the actual test vector sequence definition.
Syntax
  end pcf
Parameters
(none)
Examples
  ! in the Vector Execution section
     . . .
  unit "Data_Check"
        . . .
     pcf
         use timing set Drive_receive ! For Mux systems only
           "101011110LLLLLLHXX"
           "001100001LLLLLHLHH"
           "011100110LLLLLHHLL"
     end pcf
        . . .
  end unit
       . . .
