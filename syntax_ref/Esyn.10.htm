end compress (VCL)
Title: END OF A COMPRESS (CRC) BLOCK
The end compress function is used in a digital unit to end a compression block; that is, a block of vectors whose response states are to be added to the CRCs for that test. The block is started by the compress function.
Syntax
  end compress
Parameters
(none)
Examples
   ! in the Vector Execution section
        . . .
  unit "Compressor"
        . . .
     execute V01
     execute V02 sync
        . . .
     compress
        execute V01
        execute V02 sync
        count V03
     end compress
        . . .
  end unit
        . . .
