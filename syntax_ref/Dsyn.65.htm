div (BT-Basic)
Title: INTEGER DIVISION
The div operator performs integer division of two quantities; that is, it divides the quantity on its left by the quantity on its right, and then truncates the fractional part of the result. The priority is 6.
Syntax
  <numeric expression> div <numeric expression>
Parameters
<numeric expression>
The quantities to be operated on.
Examples
  print 38  div 7              ! prints    5
  print ‑34 div 6              ! prints    ‑5 [note that this is not the
                               ! same as int(‑34/6), which equals ‑6]
  print 3+19 div 5             ! prints 6 ( div has higher priority than + )
  print 3*18div5               ! prints 10 ( * and div have same priority )
  A = 25 \ print A div 7;A     ! prints 3 25
        ! note: if the space between "A" and "div" is omitted, an error occurs
        !       because "Adiv" looks like an identifier.
General Information
If the first expression ends with the name of a numeric variable, leave a space before typing the keyword div or an error occurs. For example, Adiv . . . (instead of A div . . .) is interpreted as a variable.
