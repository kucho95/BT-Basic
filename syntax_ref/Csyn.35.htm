compress (VCL)
Title: ADD VECTOR‑RESPONSES TO THE CRC
compress can be used in either of two ways:
• As a function in a digital unit to start a compression block. The block consists of a set of vectors whose response states are to be added to the CRCs for that test. The block is terminated by the end compress function. As is a secondary keyword used to compress individual vectors. The keyword is used by the vector execution statements—execute, preset counter and count.
• Compression causes the actual response‑bits of the executed vector to be added to the CRCs (Cyclic Redundancy Checks) for the digital test. At the end of the test, the complete compressed CRCs are compared to the CRCs originally learned from testing a known good device or circuit, to determine if the test passed or failed.
Compression cannot be used in the following constructs:
• controllerloop
• homing loop
• unit disable test
A compression block cannot contain any of the following:
• continue analog
• controllerloop
• homing loop
• subroutine call
• delay for cooling
• halt
• pause
Syntax
  compress
Parameters
(none)
Examples
  ! in the Vector Execution section
           . . .
     execute Data_1 compress     ! compresses an individual vector
           . . .
     unit "Compress vectors"
        execute V01              ! not compressed
        execute V02 sync         ! not compressed
           . . .
        compress
           execute V01
           execute V02 sync
           count V03
        end compress
           . . .
        execute V23
           . . .
     end unit
           . . .
General Information
In general, you can not use compression with anything that could give inconsistent results, because that would make the CRC indeterminate, causing the test to fail. Also, you cannot use it with anything that would pause or halt the test. However, the following would work:
  . . .
  end compress
  halt
  compress
  . . .
Refer to execute, count and preset counter for the use of compress as a keyword with those statements.
