Reference Description: Free-Field OUTPUT
The rules for outputting data in free-field format are:
• Values are output in Standard Format. Strings and numeric values are output in either regular or packed fields, by the delimiters in the output list. A comma specifies regular field, and a semicolon a packed field. The tab function a special case because it specifies where the next field will start.
• REGULAR format divides a line (80 characters) into four, fields starting at character positions (columns) 1, 41, 21, and 61.
• Outputs one item per field. Outputs an EOL (end of line) and goes to the next line when all four fields are used, or if there are no more items to output.
• Left‑justifies each item in its field and leaves remaining spaces blank.
• Numbers appear in standard or scientific format.
• Strings or numbers longer than twenty characters can occupy more than one field.
• PACKED format shortens the field to the length of the output item. The next field starts immediately to the right of the preceding field.
• After a number, leaves one blank space.
• After a string, leaves no space.
• The first regular field after a packed field can have more or less than twenty characters, depending on where that packed field ends. A regular field always ends at one of character positions 20, 40, 60, or 80.
